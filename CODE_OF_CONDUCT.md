# Code of Conduct

Henosis follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@devstrike.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by
Devstrike Digital Limited, and maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, comments and
issues that do not follow this code.
